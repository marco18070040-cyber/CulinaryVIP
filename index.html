Cooking Class & Market Tasting

Start your experience at Lucas de Gálvez Market, one of Mérida's most traditional markets. Discover local ingredients, taste regional flavors and learn about Yucatecan cuisine.

After the market visit, travel with your guide to a local home where you will participate in a hands-on cooking experience and prepare traditional Yucatecan dishes.
