Authentic Yucatecan experiences

Discover Mérida through its food

Explore the markets, streets, kitchens and traditional cantinas of Mérida with experiences designed to connect you with the authentic flavors and culture of Yucatán.

Culinary Experience

Cooking Class & Market Tasting

Start your experience at Lucas de Gálvez Market, one of Mérida's most traditional markets. Discover local ingredients, taste regional flavors and learn about the products that form the foundation of Yucatecan cuisine.

After the market visit, travel with your guide to a local home where you will participate in a hands-on cooking experience and prepare traditional Yucatecan dishes.

Experience highlights

  • ✓ Guided market tasting
  • ✓ Hands-on cooking class
  • ✓ Traditional Yucatecan dishes
  • ✓ Lunch and drinks included
  • ✓ Vegetarian and vegan options available
  • ✓ Dietary restrictions can be accommodated

Up to 12 travelers
